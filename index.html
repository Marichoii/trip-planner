    Accommodation Ideas

    • Tokyo: Asakusa / Ueno for affordable hotels and hostels
    • Kyoto: Gion / Kawaramachi for easy temple access
    • Osaka: Namba for transport and food spots

    Transport Planning

    • Get an IC card for local trains and buses.
    • Compare regional rail passes based on route, not just nationwide pass.
    • Keep airport transfer costs in the budget.

    Tourist Laws & Tax Notes

    • Always carry your passport as identification.
    • Follow local rules for trash sorting and quiet public transit etiquette.
    • Most prices include tax, but check for tax-free shopping conditions.
    • Keep receipts if claiming tax-free purchases as a visitor.
